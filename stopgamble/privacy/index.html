StopGamble — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

StopGamble is built on one principle: your recovery is nobody's dataset.

We run no servers, hold no accounts, and have no way to read anything you write in this app. This page explains exactly what that means — including the few places where data does leave your phone, because a privacy policy that only lists the reassuring parts is not a privacy policy.

What you enter stays on your device

Everything you record — quiz answers, check-ins, journal reflections, program answers, urge logs, lapse entries, streak dates, money figures, your signature and your orb collection — is stored in two places, both of which belong to you:

  • On your iPhone, in the app's local database.
  • In your private iCloud, via Apple CloudKit's private database, so your history survives a lost or replaced device.

Your private iCloud database is accessible only to your Apple ID. We are not a party to it and cannot read it. If iCloud is unavailable or disabled, the app falls back to storing data only on your device.

We collect nothing about you

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address, no phone number.
  • No advertising identifiers, no device fingerprinting, no cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell, share, rent or monetise your data — there is nothing on our side to sell.

Usage counts, measured on your device only

The app keeps a small first-party tally of how the app is used — for example how many times a screen was opened on a given day. It is stored as counts by day in a single file in the app's own storage, contains no identifiers and no content from anything you wrote, and is never transmitted anywhere. It exists so the app can be improved without watching you. Deleting the app deletes it.

Where data does leave your phone

Four narrow cases, all optional or unavoidable:

Purchases (Apple, and RevenueCat). Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage subscription status; it receives purchase and receipt information associated with a random identifier it generates. It never receives your journal, answers, streak or any recovery content. See Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy.

The DNS shield, if you turn it on. This installs an encrypted-DNS (DoH) configuration so gambling domains fail to resolve across your apps and browsers. When it is on, your device's DNS lookups are answered by Mullvad's public resolver, under their no-logging policy. Advanced users may point it at their own NextDNS configuration instead, in which case NextDNS answers those lookups under their own policy. We are not in that path and never see your browsing. Turning the shield off in iOS Settings ends this immediately.

Blocklist updates. The app periodically downloads public gambling-domain lists from jsDelivr and GitHub. As with any file download, those servers can see the request and your IP address. The request carries no personal data, no identifier, and nothing about you or your recovery.

Nothing else. The app makes no other network requests.

Notifications

All reminders — the morning program nudge, milestone notes, trial reminders — are scheduled locally by your device. They are not sent from a server and no push service receives your data. If you told us during setup that you keep your gambling private, discreet mode is enabled automatically and lock-screen wording stays neutral.

Sensitive information

What you record here concerns your health and finances, and we treat it that way. That is the reason for the design: no accounts to breach, no servers to subpoena, no analytics vendor holding a copy. The strongest protection we can offer is not holding your data at all.

Your control

  • See everything: it is all in the app — journal, timeline, collection, stats.
  • Delete everything: delete the app from your iPhone. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → StopGamble.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the Manage Subscription link in the app.

There is no "request your data" form, because there is no copy of your data on our side to send you.

Children

StopGamble is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the current version is always published at this address. Material changes will be noted in the app.
